for permission, and what to do when authorization is needed.

## What it does

- **Checks a spot**: where you are, a place you searched, a pin on the map, or
  one shared from Google Maps or Apple Maps.
- **Explains the verdict**: which zone affects you, from what height, why, with
  ENAIRE's official text in front of you.
- **Free ceiling**: how many metres you can climb without asking permission.
- **Drafts the authorization request** with your details and your drone's, ready
  for you to send from your own mail app.
- **Works without coverage**: download the area over wi-fi and it answers just
  the same out in the field, which is exactly where there is no mobile data.
- **Weather, light and shadows**: wind, visibility, sunrise, sunset, golden hour
  and blue hour for that exact spot.
- **Your profile**: your drones with their serial number and class, and a folder
  for the paperwork — certificate, insurance, declaration of conformity — with a
  warning before any of it expires.
- **Exportable flight log.**

<div class="aviso">
  <strong>This is not an official service.</strong> Zona Dron queries and
  organises information published by ENAIRE, but the legally binding source is
  the AIP and AESA's official publications. Checking before flying is the
  pilot's responsibility.
</div>

## Privacy, in one line

No personal data is collected, there are no accounts, no server of its own, and
no ads or trackers. What you save stays on your phone.

It is spelled out, service by service, in the [privacy policy](privacy).

## Installing

- **Android**: download the APK from the
  [latest release](https://github.com/DKNS-JCC/zonadron/releases/latest) and
  open it on your phone.
- **iPhone and iPad**: publishing on the App Store costs $99 a year, so an
  **unsigned** IPA is published instead, which you can sideload by signing it
  with your own free Apple ID (AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly). Instructions
  are in the [README](https://github.com/DKNS-JCC/zonadron#readme).

## Where the data comes from

| What | Source |
|---|---|
| UAS geographical zones and NOTAMs | ENAIRE |
| Terrain elevation and weather | Open-Meteo |
| Cartography and orthophotos | IGN, IDEE, PNOA |
| Place search | Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) |
| Land use and protected areas | Catastro, MITECO, IEPNB |
| Regulations | AESA, Regulations (EU) 2019/947 and 2019/945, RD 517/2024 |
