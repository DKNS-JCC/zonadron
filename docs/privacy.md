# Zona Dron privacy policy

*Last updated: 21 August 2026. Applies to Zona Dron (`es.zonadron.app`), all versions.*

**Versión en español: [política de privacidad](privacidad).**

## The short version

Zona Dron collects no personal data, has no user accounts, has no server of its
own, and carries no advertising or trackers. Everything you save in the app stays
on your phone.

## What is stored, and where

The following is stored **only in the app's private storage, on your device**. It
is not sent anywhere, neither to the developer nor to third parties:

- Your settings (default height, language, appearance, accent colour, base map).
- Your operator details: name, UAS operator number, e-mail and phone.
- Your drones: nickname, make, model, serial number, weight, class and notes.
- The documents you add (certificates, insurance, declarations of conformity…).
  The files are copied into the app's private folder.
- Your saved places, your query history and your flight log.
- The data packs you download for flying without coverage.

They are deleted when you delete them in the app, or when you uninstall it. If
you have Android backup enabled, the system may include this data in your own
device backup; that is handled by Google with your account, not by the app.

## What leaves the phone, and why

Answering "can I fly here?" means asking official services. When you run a query,
the app sends **the coordinates of the point you are checking** — and nothing
else — to these services:

| Service | What for | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| ENAIRE | UAS geographical zones and NOTAMs | Coordinates of the point |
| Open-Meteo | Weather, wind and terrain elevation | Coordinates of the point |
| Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) | Place search and place names | The search text or coordinates |
| IGN / IDEE / PNOA | Base maps and cartography | Coordinates of the map tiles |
| Catastro, MITECO, IEPNB | Land use and protected areas | Coordinates of the point |
| Google Maps / Apple Maps | Resolving a link you share with the app | The shared link |

Those requests carry no identity, no name, no device identifier, none of your
operator details and none of your documents: only the point needed for the
query. Each of those services has its own privacy policy and will see, like any
server, the IP address the request comes from.

In offline mode, with a downloaded pack, the whole check runs on the device and
nothing leaves it.

## Location

The app asks for location permission in order to check where you are. That
location is used there and then for the query and is never stored on any server.
You can use the app without granting the permission: search for the place or
mark it on the map instead.

## The documents you save

Files you add to the document folder are copied into the app's private storage on
your device. **They are never uploaded anywhere.** They only leave when you tap
"Open", which hands them to whichever app you choose to view or share them with.

## Advertising, analytics and profiling

None. No advertising, no analytics SDK, no trackers, no user profiles, no selling
data to anyone. If the app crashes, Google Play may give the developer an
anonymous crash report, as it does for any app on the store; that belongs to
Google Play and is not something the app adds.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anybody, them
included.

## Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored outside your device, there is
nothing the developer could look up, correct or delete on your behalf: the data
is yours and it is on your phone, and it is deleted from the app itself or by
uninstalling it.

## Open source

The complete source code is published and can be reviewed at
<https://github.com/DKNS-JCC/zonadron>. Anyone can check that what this document
says is what the program does.

## Changes to this policy

If it ever changes, this document will be updated with its date and the change
will be mentioned in the release notes of the corresponding version.
