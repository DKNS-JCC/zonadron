# Zona Dron terms of use

*Last updated: 24 September 2026. Apply to Zona Dron (`es.zonadron.app`), all versions.*

**Versión en español: [condiciones de uso](condiciones).**

## The short version

Zona Dron helps you check whether you can fly somewhere and prepare the
paperwork. It is not an official service, it can be wrong and it does not decide
for you: whoever flies is always the one responsible for the flight. Using the
app means accepting these terms.

<https://github.com/DKNS-JCC/zonadron/issues>.

## What it is and what it is not

- It is an **independent reference tool**. It is not an official service and is
  not affiliated with ENAIRE, AESA, Aena, the Spanish Ministry of the Interior or
  any airspace manager.
- The data comes from third parties (ENAIRE, IGN, Catastro, MITECO, Open-Meteo,

  shows it as received and interprets it as faithfully as it can, but it may be
  incomplete, out of date or unavailable.
- Data downloaded for offline use is as of the download date, which the app
  shows. Anything published afterwards is not in it.
- It **does not replace** the official AIP publication, NOTAMs, the regulations
  in force — in particular Regulations (EU) 2019/947 and 2019/945 and Royal
  Decree 517/2024 — or what the manager of each zone tells you.

## Your responsibility as a pilot

The app's verdict is **for guidance only**. Before flying, making sure the
flight is legal and safe, holding the permissions, training, registration and
insurance that apply, and flying prudently is your responsibility as pilot or
operator. If the app disagrees with an official source or with what the manager
tells you, the official source and the manager prevail.

## The documents it prepares

The app can prepare drafts of authorisation requests, aeronautical studies
(EARO), notifications and other documents from your details and from public
templates.

- They are **drafts**: you review them, complete them and, where appropriate,
  sign them. What is submitted under your name is yours.
- The app **sends nothing by itself**. E-mails open in your own mail app and you
  send them.
- Each manager has its own criteria and may ask for its own template, more
  information or a different procedure. The app does not guarantee that a
  document will be accepted, that an authorisation will be granted, or how long
  a reply will take.
